Better Enrollment is a plugin for [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com) that makes invitations the front door of your app. Run fully invite-only, hand out shareable invite links, or use invites as role and organization grants in an open app.

## Features

  <Card
    icon={<Lock />}

  <Card

  <Card

  <Card
    icon={<Link2 />}

  />
  <Card
    icon={<Building2 />}

  />
  <Card
    icon={<Users />}

  />
  <Card
    icon={<ShieldCheck />}
    title="Security first"

  />
  <Card
    icon={<ScrollText />}
    title="Audit trail"
    description="Append-only record of who invited whom and who redeemed what."
  />
  <Card
    icon={<PanelTop />}

    description="A single token page and one redeem call handle every invite kind in both modes."
