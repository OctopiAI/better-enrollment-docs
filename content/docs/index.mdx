Better Enrollment is a plugin for [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com) that makes **invitations the front door of your app**. Run fully invite-only, hand out shareable invite links, or use invites as role and organization grants in an open app.

<InstallCommand className="mt-2" />

<AtAGlance />

## Pick your front door

The plugin runs in one of two modes, auto-detected from your Better Auth config, and every invite works the same way in both.

<ModeGrid>
  <ModeCard
    href="/docs/modes"
    tag='mode: "invite-only"'

    points={[
      "Email, OAuth, and password-reset sign-ups are blocked",
      "Magic-link sign-ups are detected and guarded",
      "Redeeming an invite is what creates the account"

  />
  <ModeCard
    href="/docs/modes"

      "Same invite page, same API, no separate flow"
    ]}
  />
</ModeGrid>

## The life of an invite

A single `?token=` page and one `redeem` call handle every invite kind in both modes. The full lifecycle is on [How it works](/docs/how-it-works).

<FlowSteps>
  <FlowStep
    step="01"
    title="Create"
    description="An admin, an org member, or your server issues a private invite or a public link, with roles and org bindings attached."
  />
  <FlowStep
    step="02"
    title="Deliver"
    description="You send the link through your own email delivery or share it directly. Tokens are hashed at rest and expire on schedule."
  />
  <FlowStep
    step="03"
    title="Redeem"
    description="The invitee lands on your invite page. One call verifies the token, creates or activates the account, and writes the audit trail."
  />
</FlowSteps>
